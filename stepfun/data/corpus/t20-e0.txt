Chinese Calligraphy

Chinese calligraphy uses Chinese characters as its medium, focusing on brushwork, structure, and layout. Regular, running, cursive, clerical, and seal scripts are the five major styles. Wang Xizhi's Preface to the Orchid Pavilion is hailed as the finest running script ever. Practicing calligraphy requires long-term copying of model works to grasp the charm of classical brushwork.
